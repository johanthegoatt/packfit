+ Cabin liquids (the 3-1-1 rule)

Every bottle 100 ml (3.4 oz) or less, and they all fit one small clear bag. It's the size printed on the bottle that counts, not how full it is.

Separate the sizes with commas. Medicines and baby food are usually allowed over the limit but may be checked on their own, so leave them out here. Rules vary by country and change, so check your airport before you fly.
